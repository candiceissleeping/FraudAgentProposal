## 逐項理由

**B01 — 45**
- C 判斷性營收 +10："the Company recognized revenues from optical capacity asset sales in the amount of $1.013 billion and $468 million"；"In some cases, [COMPANY] enters into two transactions that occur at the same time: one to sell IRUs to companies…"（同時買賣的交換交易）
- C 有利的會計變更 +5："Dex changed to the 'point of publication method'… resulted in an increase in net income of $240 million"
- C 非 GAAP / 併購成長 +5：反覆以 "adjusted EBITDA" 說明成長，"due primarily to the Merger"
- D 政府調查 +10："the staff of the SEC has conducted an investigation concerning its earnings release… The staff has decided to recommend that the SEC authorize an action"
- D 證券詐欺訴訟 +5："allege… made material false statements… in violation of sections 10(b) and 20(a)… due to the alleged failure to disclose four transactions between [COMPANY] and KMC"
- E 流動性 +10："RECENT DEVELOPMENTS IMPACTING LIQUIDITY AND CAPITAL RESOURCES. Until February 2002, [COMPANY] maintained commercial paper programs"（商業本票融資已中止）
- 未計分：「SEC 若不同意，可能需要重編」屬可能性，非實際重編；KPNQwest 為 47.5% 持股關係企業，非內部人控制。

**B02 — 0**：Item 9A 有效；Item 3 僅一般訴訟；漲價、流動性只出現在一般風險因素。

**B03 — 3**

- 未計分：捐款 5,000 萬美元給公司基金會（"a related-party not-for-profit organization"）— 透明揭露，非內部人控制實體交易。

- 未計分：關係人應付款 595 萬美元（前股東、現員工控制的 LLC）金額不重大；稽核委員會內部調查不屬「政府」調查（規則限制，見結論）。
