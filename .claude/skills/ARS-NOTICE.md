`deep-research`, `sr-screener`, plus `.claude/agents/screening_reviewer_agent.md`, are copied
unmodified from Academic Research Skills by Cheng-I Wu:

- Source: https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills

Installed with the upstream "skills-copy" method (docs/SETUP.md, Method 1). The plugin-only
parts (slash commands, SessionStart/PreToolUse hooks, repo-level `shared/` and `scripts/`)
are not included.

To update: re-clone upstream and replace these folders with `cp -R`.
